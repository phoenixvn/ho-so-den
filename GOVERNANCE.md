# Governance

## Maintainers

The initial repository steward is **@realitechteam**. Contributors retain
copyright over their contributions. Maintainers manage releases, review access,
roadmap decisions and community moderation; they do not gain ownership of users'
privately stored archives.

## Decisions

- Small fixes: reviewed pull request, relevant tests and license checks.
- Significant features: issue or discussion before implementation.
- Storage formats, authentication, contribution protocol and licensing changes:
  written design proposal documenting compatibility and privacy implications.
- Seek consensus; maintainers explain final decisions and unresolved trade-offs.

Decisions and release notes are recorded publicly. Never include private user
content or operational secrets in decision records.

## Becoming a maintainer

Sustained constructive contributions, sound reviews and reliable community
participation can lead to an invitation. Responsibilities and access are agreed
explicitly; publishing packages or operating community infrastructure is not
automatically granted with code-review access.

## Releases

Pre-alpha releases may change formats and APIs. A release must clearly identify
implemented vs planned features. Stable compatibility guarantees will be defined
before a production release. No DAO, token-weighted governance or public vote on
whether a person is guilty is part of this project.
