# Community Code of Conduct

We want a community where people can build, question and learn respectfully,
regardless of background, identity, nationality, experience or ability.

## Expected behavior

- Critique ideas and code, not people. Be specific and constructive.
- Assume room for correction and acknowledge mistakes.
- Respect privacy, consent and the right to step away from a conversation.
- Keep discussions relevant to the project. Use fictional examples for case data.
- Make participation accessible to Vietnamese and English speakers where possible.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, unwanted sexual attention, doxxing,
impersonation and coordinated disruption are not acceptable. Do not use project
spaces to accuse or target identifiable people or to publish private records.

## Scope and moderation

These rules apply to project issues, PRs, discussions and official community
spaces, and to conduct when representing the project. Maintainers may clarify,
edit, hide or remove content and warn, temporarily restrict or remove participants.
Responses should be proportionate to the behavior and its impact.

## Reporting and review

For a non-sensitive incident, open a moderation request in Discussions without
repeating private information. To request a private contact route, open a minimal
issue titled “Private moderation contact requested” containing no incident details;
maintainers will arrange a private channel. Never post evidence publicly just to
obtain attention. Security vulnerabilities use [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

Maintainers will handle reports with discretion and recuse themselves when
personally involved. Appeals may request review by another available maintainer.
This small volunteer project does not promise an emergency response service.
